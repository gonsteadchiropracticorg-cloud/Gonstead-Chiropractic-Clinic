Origins and Contributions by Dr. Clarence S. Gonstead

Clarence Selmer Gonstead (1898-1978) was a pivotal figure in chiropractic, transforming it from a rudimentary practice into a more scientifically grounded biomechanical discipline.

Key Innovation: The Gonstead Disc Concept

Gonstead developed the "Gonstead Disc Concept," which posited that the vertebral disc was often the primary cause of nerve pressure in subluxations.

Core Principles & Diagnostic Tools

The Gonstead Chiropractic Technique uses a five-component diagnostic framework:

  • Full-Spine Weight-Bearing X-rays: 14x36 inch films for comprehensive assessment
  • Static & Motion Palpation: Physical examination of spine at rest and in motion
  • Nervoscope/Thermography: Heat differential detection along the spine
  • Visual Assessment: Posture, gait, and body symmetry observation
  • Symptom/Case Management: Integration of patient symptoms with findings
