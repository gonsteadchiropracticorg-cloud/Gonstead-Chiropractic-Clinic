Understanding Your Spine

Your spine is the foundation of your health - learn how the Gonstead technique restores its natural function

Spinal Anatomy

Foundation of Health

Your spine consists of 24 movable vertebrae, each playing a crucial role in protecting your nervous system and maintaining your body's structural integrity.

Key Components:

  • Cervical Spine: 7 vertebrae (C1-C7) - Your neck region
  • Thoracic Spine: 12 vertebrae (T1-T12) - Your mid-back
  • Lumbar Spine: 5 vertebrae (L1-L5) - Your lower back

Nervous System

Body's Communication Network

Your nervous system controls every function in your body. When vertebrae misalign, they can interfere with nerve communication, affecting your health.

Each spinal level controls specific organs and body functions

Gonstead Analysis

Precision Diagnosis

The Gonstead technique uses five comprehensive diagnostic tools to identify the exact location and nature of spinal problems.

Five-Component Analysis
  1. X-ray Analysis: Full-spine weight-bearing films reveal structural alignment
  2. Static Palpation: Feeling for inflammation, swelling, and tissue changes
  3. Motion Palpation: Assessing joint movement and restrictions
  4. Instrumentation (Nervoscope): Detecting heat differentials indicating nerve interference
  5. Visual Assessment: Observing posture, gait, and body symmetry

The Gonstead Technique

Precision adjustments based on scientific analysis and biomechanical principles

Proper Posture

Daily Habits Matter

Good posture reduces stress on your spine and helps maintain the corrections achieved through treatment.

Posture Tips:

  • Keep your head directly over your shoulders
  • Maintain natural spinal curves
  • Take regular breaks from sitting
  • Use ergonomic workstation setup
